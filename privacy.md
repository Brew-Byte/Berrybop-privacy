# Privacy Policy

- **Effective date:** May 22, 2026
- **App:** Berrybop (iOS / iPadOS)
- **Developer:** Preetinder Marok

## Summary

Berrybop does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. The app runs entirely on your device, has no user accounts,
no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## Information We Collect

**None.** Berrybop does not collect any data of any kind, including but not
limited to:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Photos, microphone, or camera input
- Usage analytics, crash logs, or behavioral data
- Drawings, audio, or any content created in the app

Drawings made in the Paint activity are kept on your device only. They are
not uploaded anywhere.

## Third Parties

Berrybop contains **no third-party SDKs**, no advertising networks, and no
analytics services. The app does not communicate with any server.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA & PIPEDA)

Berrybop is designed for children under 13 and complies with the
**Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States
and the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act
(PIPEDA)** in Canada.

Because Berrybop collects no personal information from anyone — including
children — there is no data to access, modify, or delete. Parents who have
questions may contact us at the address below.

## Parental Gate

Any feature that could leave the app (such as opening this Privacy Policy
or contacting support from inside the app) is gated behind a math problem
that only a parent or older child can solve, in accordance with Apple's
App Review Guideline 1.3.

## In-App Purchases and Ads

Berrybop has **no in-app purchases** and **no advertising**.

## Permissions

Berrybop does not request access to the camera, microphone, photo library,
location, contacts, notifications, or any other sensitive iOS permission.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app
adds a feature that handles data — the new policy will be posted here and
the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be
noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.

## Contact
